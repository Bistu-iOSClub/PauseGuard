---
name: new-research
description: Create a PauseGuard technical research note comparing implementation options. Use when an unresolved technical question needs evidence before a decision or ADR.
---

Save to `docs/research/YYYY-MM-topic.md` and include:

- problem and constraints
- current code facts
- at least two credible options when alternatives exist
- comparison criteria and evidence
- recommendation
- risks, mitigations, and validation plan

Prefer Apple documentation, WWDC sessions, and primary library documentation. Separate sourced facts from inference. Convert accepted, durable architectural choices into an ADR.
