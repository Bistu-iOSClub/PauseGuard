---
name: dev-log
description: Maintain the PauseGuard development log. Use at a meaningful milestone, handoff, blocked state, or end-of-session checkpoint.
---

Write or update `docs/dev/logs/<github-id>-YYYY-MM-DD.md` for the current team member:

```markdown
# <GitHub ID> — YYYY-MM-DD

## 阶段状态

## 完成

## 进行中

## 阻塞

## 下一步

- [ ] ...

## 备注
```

Record outcomes and durable context, not every command. Merge multiple same-day updates for the same member. Suggest an ADR or fix-history entry when the work exposes a durable decision or reusable failure pattern.
