---
name: fix-history
description: Record a reusable PauseGuard failure and its verified fix. Use after resolving a non-obvious build, runtime, tooling, or regression issue likely to recur.
---

Save `docs/dev/fixes/YYYY-MM-DD-short-description.md` with:

- 日期和错误现象
- 复现条件与证据
- 直接根因和促成因素
- 最小修复及其取舍
- 验证结果
- 预防或更早发现方式
- 相关文件、issue、PR 或 commit（存在时）

Do not create an entry for trivial typos or one-off local environment noise.
